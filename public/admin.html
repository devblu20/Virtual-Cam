How to read this dashboardDurations & statuses ＋

What counts as video time?

Time is estimated from activity reports, usually about 20 seconds apart. Gaps longer than 45 seconds are excluded. “No measured time” means no timed interval was recorded—not proof that the camera was never used. These records are not billing or attendance evidence.

What do statuses mean?

Activity reported: the client is sending recent reports. Finished: video activity was reported before the session ended. No video confirmed: the request had no confirmed activity. Reports stopped: updates are missing; a disconnect is not verified. Connection failed: the connection attempt failed.

What is saved?

Account ID, avatar details, platform, timestamps and approximate duration—not meeting recordings. Shared personal keys share an account ID. Only updated clients report usage, and older activity cannot be reconstructed.
